Keep X and Y tick labels inside the pad and apart from each other.

First lowers the number of primary divisions of a linear axis whose numeric labels are closer than half a text height to each other (secondary divisions are kept; set SetNdivisions afterwards to override). Widens the right margin when the last X label would be clipped at the pad edge. When the first X label and the lowest Y label overlap at the bottom-left corner, moves the X labels down and the Y labels left by the same distance, with titles and margins following. Called by SetYAxis (and so SetAxes) after the axes are styled.
